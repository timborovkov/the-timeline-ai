# Timeline Prospect Brief

Related: [Prospect deck](./prospect-deck.html).

## Headline

Keep your current tools. Timeline turns them into cited project and client
memory.

## The Problem

Your team already did the work, but someone still has to reconstruct it:

- What changed?
- What did we promise?
- Who owns the next step?
- What should this stakeholder know?
- What context does a teammate need before joining?
- Where is the source?

That answer usually lives across Slack, meetings, docs, tasks, email, and
someone's memory.

## The Timeline

Timeline captures work as it happens and generates cited updates, daily
digests, handoffs, and answers from the evidence.

It starts beside the tools your team already uses and is designed around the
places work already happens.

## Ingest Surfaces And Integrations

Timeline should meet the team wherever work happens. Durable event history comes
through capture surfaces, first-party integrations, and ingest webhooks; MCP
extends the agent into long-tail systems for live access.

- **Slack and Telegram:** conversations, files, voice notes, forwarded messages,
  channel context, `/ask`, and `@Timeline` answers.
- **Meetings:** transcript capture for Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom.
- **Email:** forward, CC, or BCC to a team address.
- **Documents and knowledge:** uploads, document versions, extracted text,
  chunks, citations, captured-file promotion, Google Drive, Notion, and
  Confluence.
- **Calendar:** event rows, approvals, and subscription feeds.
- **Web/PWA:** typed notes, uploads, and direct capture.
- **Project and task work:** Linear, Jira, Asana, Monday.com, Trello, Basecamp.
- **Engineering and operations:** Sentry, Datadog, incidents, alerts, releases,
  and deploy markers.
- **Code and delivery:** GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, releases, pull requests,
  commits, CI, and deployments.
- **CRM and pipeline systems:** HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio, Close.
- **Support and success:** Zendesk, Intercom.
- **Live tools:** custom MCP servers, internal tools, and long-tail SaaS.

The bar for an integration is more than "the agent can call a tool." Timeline's
model is durable history, selected resources, backfill, incremental sync, cited
raw events, and object mapping where useful.

## What You Get

- **Updates:** answer stakeholder questions without rewriting status from
  scratch.
- **Digests:** see what changed and what needs attention.
- **Handoffs:** bring someone into a project or client account quickly.
- **Answers:** ask what happened and see citations.
- **Operational memory:** approved project, account, commitment, task, and
  decision state derived from event history.

## Why It Is Different

- Every claim is cited back to source evidence.
- Raw events stay immutable.
- Durable memory is approval-backed.
- Team and per-event visibility are enforced by the data model.
- No migration is required to try it on one real project.

## Pilot

Start with one client, account, or project.

Connect the tools your team already uses. Timeline will generate the next
update, digest, handoff, and answer from the evidence.

Success looks like:

> Stakeholders trust the generated update because every claim has a source.
