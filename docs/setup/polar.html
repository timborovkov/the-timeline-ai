Polar billing setup

Timeline uses Polar as merchant of record for Pay as you go, Team, and Business subscriptions, plus native usage meters invoiced monthly in arrears. Customer charges stay in shadow billing until BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=true.

Environments

  • Sandbox dashboard: sandbox.polar.sh · API https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1
  • Production dashboard: polar.sh · API https://api.polar.sh/v1

Sandbox accounts, tokens, products, and meters are fully isolated from production. Create a Timeline-specific access token; do not reuse Vernix / Irmin product or webhook IDs even when they share a Polar organization. Create the Pay as you go, Team, and Business products and the Timeline meters in the sandbox dashboard, then paste those ids below. pnpm billing:polar-setup (optional --env-file= and --webhook-url=) archives any active Timeline Top-up product and does not create one. It creates or reuses Timeline Enterprise (a custom platform fee plus the six metered prices) and writes POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_ENTERPRISE. It ensures a Timeline webhook, then writes POLAR_WEBHOOK_ID and POLAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET and removes a retired POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_TOPUP assignment. It refuses to update or delete endpoints for another product, including an Irmin host or /api/v1/webhooks/polar. It deletes only a disabled example.com/api/webhooks/polar placeholder. Railway deploys main to production and the staging branch to staging, so a feature branch has no preview URL. The command defaults the webhook to https://thetimeline.cc/api/webhooks/polar. That route has to be deployed before Polar deliveries succeed.

Environment variables

POLAR_ACCESS_TOKEN=
POLAR_SERVER=sandbox
POLAR_ORGANIZATION_ID=
POLAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET=
POLAR_WEBHOOK_ID=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_PAYG=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_TEAM=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_BUSINESS=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_ENTERPRISE=
POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID=
POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE=
BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=false
PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAILS=

PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAILS is a comma-separated allowlist for /app/admin/billing and pnpm billing:operator. It is not a team role. Comped workspaces keep metering and the spend cap, skip Polar checkout and meter ingest, and show a one-line comp banner on the team billing page. Customer billing shows the plan, this month's spend against the cap, and the next invoice; usage is a meter table. Pricing detail lives on /pricing. Members only see a cap or allowance notice. Assigning a comp revokes the team's current Polar subscription immediately so the platform fee stops. If that subscription exists and Polar is not configured, the comp is refused and nothing is saved. Price overrides (platform fee, included members, per-member price, metered discount percent) and a per-team Polar discount id are optional and can expire. Every change is appended to billing_operator_audit.

Products and meters (v1)

  • Timeline Pay as you go — €0 subscription. Checkout saves the card. Usage above the Free floor is ingested and invoiced monthly.
  • Timeline Team — €49/mo + up to €60 included usage discount
  • Timeline Business — €199/mo + up to €250 included usage discount
  • Timeline Enterprise — custom monthly platform fee (minimum €0) plus the same six metered prices. A platform operator opens the checkout from /app/admin/billing or pnpm billing:operator -- start-enterprise.

There is no prepaid wallet and no top-up product. pnpm billing:polar-setup archives a sandbox product named Timeline Top-up if one is still active.

Native Polar meters (event name → sum units):

  • timeline_ai_eur_cents — customer AI charge in euro cents
  • timeline_recall_minutes
  • timeline_email_units
  • timeline_storage_gb_month
  • timeline_accepted_sources
  • timeline_extra_member_days — extra active members, summed as fractions of a member-month

Extra active members beyond the included count accrue as member_days on the local spend cap and are ingested daily as timeline_extra_member_days. The operation id is member_days:<team>:<day>:<user>, so a replay does not double-charge. The Polar price on Pay as you go, Team, Business, and Enterprise is €2.00 per member-month (unit_amount 200 cents). Payable cents are converted to member-month units at that catalog price, so a custom extra-member price or metered-discount percent changes the units rather than replacing the shared price. A Polar subscription discount is not used for this, because it would also cut the platform fee and the other meters. Included-usage discount is not spent on extra members. Seat quantity is not used: Polar prorates a seat from the moment the quantity changes, which does not match a daily census, a member who leaves, or a per-team price. pnpm billing:polar-setup creates the meter and attaches the price without dropping the other product prices. Comps and shadow billing still count the charge on the spend cap and do not ingest it. An empty Enterprise included-member count means the roster is included. Extra members are billed only after the operator sets both an included count and an extra-member price.

Polar ingest requires a matching active subscription whose product actually prices that meter, including Enterprise. A matching product id is not enough: Pay as you go, Team, and Business setup only guarantees the extra-member price, so a missing native meter price stays on the local ledger and opens one billing_unbilled_alerts row per team, meter, and month. Operators see open alerts for the selected team on /app/admin/billing. Shadow billing, comps, Free allowance usage, and sub-cent rounds are not ingested and do not alert. A cent that accumulates from those sub-cent rounds is ingested as the catalog units that price it (four email units, or twenty accepted sources, per cent). A percentage discount is taken from the cumulative meter total before that cent is rounded. A later ingest of that meter resolves the open alert. past_due, payment_retry, and read_only still cover the meter when the subscription product prices it.

Agent turns, concurrent Recall bots, custom MCP servers, documents, storage, indexed chunks, and active-member ceilings are plan capacity enforced in product code. Do not add Polar meters for them. /app/usage and /pricing keep those ceilings in a closed Infrastructure limits disclosure rather than the default billed view. Team Billing does not repeat them. Webhook and search bursts stay on Redis token buckets; unique ingest volume is accepted_sources. A person may claim one Free grant after email verification; failed claims retry on the next signed-in load. Extra owned workspaces are restricted until a payment method is added. Unverified owners are not granted Free by the backfill migration. Removed owner memberships cannot claim or steal that person-level grant.

Background workers attach a billing context so OpenRouter wrappers settle the ai meter from exact usage.cost. Ask and Recall keep their own reserve/settle path and skip the wrapper meter to avoid double-counting.

Webhook

Point a Polar webhook at POST /api/webhooks/polar with Standard Webhooks / whsec_ signing. Subscribe at least to subscription, order, and refund events (order.paid, order.refunded, refund.created, refund.updated, plus subscription.created, subscription.active, subscription.updated, subscription.canceled, and subscription.revoked). pnpm billing:polar-setup creates that endpoint. Keep it pointed at a deploy that serves this route; production and staging only follow main and staging. Webhook signatures require a timestamp within five minutes. Subscription events compare Polar modified_at to polar_event_modified_at, not local updated_at, after locking the billing account in the same transaction. Request bodies are bounded to the integration webhook size limit before signature verify. Paid-plan activation writes the catalog default spend cap (€25/€100/€500) when the workspace is still on Free; later subscription updates preserve an administrator-selected paid cap, including €0 as a hard stop. Settlement that exceeds the paid spend cap still records the full ledger charge and freezes the workspace. Raising a positive spend cap restores read_only or balance_exhausted to the plan's active state. An active subscription update does not clear that local spend-cap freeze, and a historical wallet shortfall row does not re-freeze an active account. past_due and payment_retry stay paused until a later subscription webhook returns an active state. Entering past_due emails owners once per UTC month. Extra member-days persist a denied charge and freeze rather than skipping the extra seat. Janitor member-day ticks retry unsettled facts across the full 31-day lookback, including the previous calendar month, after billing recovery. A missed day with no member-day row yet uses the plan effective that day, then the current plan. Terminal Recall no-shows settle elapsed waiting-room minutes; a retry still releases so the same meeting can reserve again. Cancelling a joining/active meeting with no transcript chunks settles elapsed waiting-room minutes instead of releasing them. Postmark success is recorded before email settlement. A delivered message whose settlement fails stays reserved for the janitor and is not resent. Reserve, settle, release, and reservation expiry lock the billing account before the reservation row. Meeting-finalize billing uses the system actor so a departed creator cannot skip settlement. Free-grant inserts use ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING so a unique race cannot abort team-create. The post-verify Free-grant backfill prefers oldest restricted Free workspaces and never assigns the grant to a paid team. Scheduled join billing denials fail the occurrence without pausing auto-join as consecutive failures. Document uploads are blocked in every non-reservable billing state. order.paid does not credit a local balance. A stray paid order for the retired Timeline Top-up, or any other one-time purchase that is not a subscription product, is recorded on the usage ledger and opens an unbilled alert. The wallet balance stays unchanged. Included invoice discount is collected locally and is not ingested. Polar receives the customer-payable remainder, with native units scaled by that remainder, including sub-cent rounds that stay local when invoice cents are 0. PAYG, Team, Business, and Enterprise meter events are ingested when charges are live, the workspace is not comped, and the active subscription product matches that plan and prices the meter. Otherwise the charge stays local and an operator alert is opened. Comps are not ingested. Polar ingest uses a ledger outbox that claims a row before ingesting, Polar receives a stable event id, and a failed ingest does not undo a committed charge. Janitor included-discount resets lock the billing account so they cannot overwrite a newer Polar plan or period, and a missed renewal advances by calendar months from the subscription anchor. Ask streams that abort after work starts settle at least the reserved customer charge. Failed structured-output attempts still contribute OpenRouter usage.cost. Recall minutes that cross a UTC month are attributed to each month they span. Document create serializes capacity under advisory lock key 1; restore takes key 1 then key 2 so restored chunks serialize with extraction writes. Worker AI reservations count against plan costlyWorkerConcurrency and delay the job when busy. Background AI metering keeps the reservation when settle fails after a successful provider call and reuses the BullMQ job id. A thrown call that already reported OpenRouter usage settles that attempt, then releases the reservation. Each transcription chunk gets its own operation id. Aborting a Recall join after the bot is accepted keeps the reservation when leave fails so the janitor can retry. Terminal Recall settlement errors propagate so the status webhook can 503 and retry. Re-accepting a removed member stamps a new membership createdAt and appends the previous interval to prior_intervals. A leftover Free team stays unrestricted while another owner's grant remains assigned. Storage snapshots backfill missed days across the 31-day lookback, price each day on the plan in force then, and count that day in its own month. A prior month is recorded on that month and is not compared to the live spend cap. Only a positive charge in the current UTC period can freeze on that cap. Restoring a document keeps the closed absence interval so those days are not billed again. Slack/Telegram/MCP Ask and web chat save the completed cost before settle and keep the reservation when settlement fails so the janitor can retry it. Paid-plan changes reuse the Polar subscription in non-active paid states. Extra-member plan preview prorates from period member-days, including person-days when the current plan has no extras. Inbound email audio skipped for a denied email meter is flushed by the janitor when billing can reserve again. Denied inbound-email parent enrichment is stamped billing_enrichment_deferred and flushed on the email meter, not the accepted-source meter. Deferred accepted-source flush rotates past still-blocked rows; a replay after an insert crash meters the existing dedup row. Seat claims are blocked while billing is paused. The spend cap counts the locally metered charge for Pay as you go, Team, Business, and every comp, including an expired comp whose entitlements are Free. Included credit that zeros the invoice does not skip the cap. Open reservations count toward it. Standard Free stays on the allowance. Standard Enterprise settlement does not freeze on the spend cap. A comped Enterprise workspace still enforces its spend cap. An expired Enterprise comp uses the Free allowance and still enforces the cap. Enterprise checkout is opened by a platform operator and is not self-serve. A team has one outstanding checkout. An earlier session is expired when Polar reports it expired, failed, or missing. Polar has no merchant expire endpoint, so a session that stays open, or one that already started payment, blocks another link. The new link stores only its own pending terms and leaves the current plan in place until the paid subscription webhook for that checkout. If a second live subscription activates, Timeline revokes the extra Polar subscription, or opens an unbilled alert when the revoke fails, and names that subscription id so a duplicate charge can be refunded. Paying that checkout stores the contracted platform fee apart from the usage override. Clearing or replacing the usage override leaves the fee in place. That fee is used only while the effective plan is Enterprise. A confirmed subscription cancel clears the Polar subscription id and the contracted fee, and an Enterprise cancel also clears the usage override. Plan preview applies a metered percentage to usage and to extra-member cents before included credit. Recall reservations, capacity, and included-discount refills follow the effective plan, so an expired comp uses the Free minute cap rather than the stored comp plan. Polar price coverage is read only when a settlement is payable, and that product's priced events are reused for later settlements. Replayed member-days keep the historical plan's extra-member rate. Enterprise checkout does not clear a canceled subscription id and does not copy an expired price override into those terms. Platform-fee overrides are not saved on Pay as you go, Team, or Business; the Enterprise checkout sends that fee to Polar. Clearing a comp refuses while a Polar subscription id remains, then restores unrestricted Free only when this workspace holds the Free grant. Background AI metering skips OpenRouter when the durable operation is already settled or the reservation is reused with provider_completed. A successful worker call stamps worker_settlement_pending before settle. The janitor drains that marker, including an already-expired row, before the reservation TTL, and a retry keeps the completed result. Member-day ledger rows stamp plan_id; later ticks reuse that day's included-member count. A missed day with no stamp uses the plan effective that day. Storage-cap finalization deletes the uploaded object when admission fails. Captured Slack/Telegram/email attachments assert document and storage capacity. Free in-allowance usage is not invoiced when live charging is enabled. Janitor Recall leave retries run before reservation expiry, and expiry skips rows with pending_recall_leave_bot_id. Interactive search embeddings run under billing ALS. The Free grant unassigns when its owner leaves or is demoted. Member-day janitor ticks backfill missed days. Deleted documents are excluded from indexed-chunk capacity; restore counts those chunks. Web chat settles known compression cost when the answer stream fails. BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=false is a process-wide shadow kill switch. Set BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED on document-extract-orchestrator as well as web/worker; it is a non-secret allowlisted toggle, not an extra credential.

Public and app surfaces

  • Public catalog: /pricing
  • Team usage: /app/usage. Owners and admins see the meter table. Other members see which Free allowance is used up, without the meter table or euro amounts.
  • Team settings: /app/team?section=billing (owner/admin) — plan, PAYG checkout (a €0 Polar subscription that saves the card; usage above the Free floor is invoiced monthly), Team and Business checkout on the same subscription, spend cap (hard stop and 50/75/90/100 alerts), and cheapest-plan preview only when another self-serve plan saves at least €5 and 10% of the current projected bill. Selecting another paid plan updates the existing Polar subscription (or the customer portal) instead of opening a second checkout, including when the workspace is past due, in payment retry, or read-only. past_due and payment_retry show a pause banner and an Update payment method button that opens the Polar customer portal. Enterprise workspaces do not show PAYG/Team/Business checkout buttons. Submitted Polar discount codes must match POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE before POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID is attached.
  • Owner emails: spend-cap 50/75/90/100%, Free near-limit / exhaustion, and subscription past_due (billing_usage_alert via Postmark; once per threshold or past-due notice per period)
