Polar billing setup

Timeline uses Polar as merchant of record for optional Team / Business subscriptions, pay-as-you-go checkout, and native usage meters. Customer charges stay in shadow billing until BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=true.

Environments

  • Sandbox dashboard: sandbox.polar.sh · API https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1
  • Production dashboard: polar.sh · API https://api.polar.sh/v1

Sandbox accounts, tokens, products, and meters are fully isolated from production. Create a Timeline-specific access token; do not reuse Vernix / Irmin product or webhook IDs even when they share a Polar organization.

POLAR_ACCESS_TOKEN=
POLAR_SERVER=sandbox
POLAR_ORGANIZATION_ID=

Products and meters (v1)

  • Timeline Pay as you go — €0 platform fee + metered overage
  • Timeline Team — €49/mo + up to €60 included usage discount
  • Timeline Business — €199/mo + up to €250 included usage discount
  • Timeline prepaid top-up — one-time €10 wallet credit (first PAYG collection)

Native Polar meters (event name → sum units):

  • timeline_ai_eur_cents — customer AI charge in euro cents
  • timeline_recall_minutes
  • timeline_email_units
  • timeline_storage_gb_month
  • timeline_accepted_sources

Extra active members are billed as member_days on the workspace invoice (€2 per member-month, prorated). That meter is not ingested to Polar.

Background workers attach a billing context so OpenRouter wrappers settle the ai meter from exact usage.cost. Ask and Recall keep their own reserve/settle path and skip the wrapper meter to avoid double-counting.

Webhook

Point a Polar webhook at POST /api/webhooks/polar with Standard Webhooks / whsec_ signing. Subscribe at least to subscription and order events. Keep the endpoint disabled until the URL is publicly reachable (local tunnel or deploy).

Public and app surfaces

  • Public catalog: /pricing
  • Team usage: /app/usage
  • Team settings: /app/team?section=billing (owner/admin) — plan, €10 top-up, auto-reload, cheapest-plan preview, spend cap
  • Owner emails: spend-cap 50/75/90/100% and Free near-limit / exhaustion (billing_usage_alert via Postmark; once per threshold per period)
