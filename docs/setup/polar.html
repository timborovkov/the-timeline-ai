Polar billing setup

Timeline uses Polar as merchant of record for optional Team / Business subscriptions, pay-as-you-go checkout, and native usage meters. Customer charges stay in shadow billing until BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=true.

Environments

  • Sandbox dashboard: sandbox.polar.sh · API https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1
  • Production dashboard: polar.sh · API https://api.polar.sh/v1

Sandbox accounts, tokens, products, and meters are fully isolated from production. Create a Timeline-specific access token; do not reuse Vernix / Irmin product or webhook IDs even when they share a Polar organization.

Products and meters (v1)

  • Timeline Pay as you go — €0 platform fee + metered overage
  • Timeline Team — €49/mo + up to €60 included usage discount
  • Timeline Business — €199/mo + up to €250 included usage discount
  • Timeline prepaid top-up — one-time €10 wallet credit (first PAYG collection)

Native Polar meters (event name → sum units):

  • timeline_ai_eur_cents — customer AI charge in euro cents
  • timeline_recall_minutes
  • timeline_email_units
  • timeline_storage_gb_month
  • timeline_accepted_sources

Extra active members accrue as member_days on the prepaid wallet ledger (€2 per member-month) and do not consume Team/Business included-usage discount. Daily janitor charges settle each extra member once per day and use a cumulative monthly cent delta so rounding still totals €2. That meter is not ingested to Polar and is not a Polar invoice line.

Agent turns, concurrent Recall bots, custom MCP servers, documents, storage, indexed chunks, and active-member ceilings are plan capacity enforced in product code. Do not add Polar meters for them. /app/usage, Billing settings, and /pricing keep those ceilings in a closed Infrastructure limits disclosure rather than the default billed view. Webhook and search bursts stay on Redis token buckets; unique ingest volume is accepted_sources. A person may claim one Free grant after email verification; failed claims retry on the next signed-in load. Extra owned workspaces are restricted until a payment method is added. Unverified owners are not granted Free by the backfill migration. Removed owner memberships cannot claim or steal that person-level grant.

Background workers attach a billing context so OpenRouter wrappers settle the ai meter from exact usage.cost. Ask and Recall keep their own reserve/settle path and skip the wrapper meter to avoid double-counting.

Webhook

Point a Polar webhook at POST /api/webhooks/polar with Standard Webhooks / whsec_ signing. Subscribe at least to subscription, order, and refund events (order.paid, order.refunded, refund.created, refund.updated). Keep the endpoint disabled until the URL is publicly reachable (local tunnel or deploy). Webhook signatures require a timestamp within five minutes. Subscription events compare Polar modified_at to polar_event_modified_at, not local updated_at, after locking the billing account in the same transaction. Request bodies are bounded to the integration webhook size limit before signature verify. Paid-plan activation writes the catalog default spend cap (€25/€100/€500) when the workspace is still on Free; later subscription updates preserve an administrator-selected paid cap, including €0 as a hard stop. Settlement that exceeds the reserved wallet or paid spend cap still records the full ledger charge and freezes the workspace. Document uploads are blocked in every non-reservable billing state. Refunds of the €10 top-up debit the prepaid wallet, serialize concurrent refund.created / order.refunded totals, and freeze the workspace if that credit was already spent. Refunds that arrive before order.paid are stored and applied when the top-up credits. Local usage settlement is independent of Polar ingest: prepaid wallet and included discount already collect locally, so those units are not sent to Polar meters. Remaining Polar ingest uses a ledger outbox that claims a row before ingesting, Polar receives a stable event id, and a failed ingest does not undo a committed charge. Janitor included-discount resets lock the billing account so they cannot overwrite a newer Polar plan or period. Ask streams that abort after work starts settle at least the reserved customer charge. Failed structured-output attempts still contribute OpenRouter usage.cost. Recall minutes that cross a UTC month are attributed to each month they span. Document create and restore serialize capacity under advisory lock key 1. Deferred accepted-source flush rotates past still-blocked rows. Set BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED on document-extract-orchestrator as well as web/worker; it is a non-secret allowlisted toggle, not an extra credential.

Public and app surfaces

  • Public catalog: /pricing
  • Team usage: /app/usage
  • Team settings: /app/team?section=billing (owner/admin) — plan, €10 top-up on wallet-backed plans, auto-reload Polar checkout email (skipped unless remaining spend-cap headroom covers the full €10 product, including a €0 spend cap), cheapest-plan preview, spend cap. Selecting another paid plan updates the existing Polar subscription (or the customer portal) instead of opening a second checkout. Submitted Polar discount codes must match POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE before POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID is attached.
  • Owner emails: spend-cap 50/75/90/100% and Free near-limit / exhaustion (billing_usage_alert via Postmark; once per threshold per period)
