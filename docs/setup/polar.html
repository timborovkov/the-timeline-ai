Polar billing setup

Timeline uses Polar as merchant of record for Pay as you go, Team, and Business subscriptions, plus native usage meters invoiced monthly in arrears. Customer charges stay in shadow billing until BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=true.

Environments

  • Sandbox dashboard: sandbox.polar.sh · API https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1
  • Production dashboard: polar.sh · API https://api.polar.sh/v1

Sandbox accounts, tokens, products, and meters are fully isolated from production. Create a Timeline-specific access token; do not reuse Vernix / Irmin product or webhook IDs even when they share a Polar organization. Create the Pay as you go, Team, and Business products and the five Timeline meters in the sandbox dashboard, then paste those ids below. pnpm billing:polar-setup (optional --env-file= and --webhook-url=) archives any active Timeline Top-up product and does not create one. It ensures a Timeline webhook, then writes POLAR_WEBHOOK_ID and POLAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET and removes a retired POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_TOPUP assignment. It refuses to update or delete endpoints for another product, including an Irmin host or /api/v1/webhooks/polar. It deletes only a disabled example.com/api/webhooks/polar placeholder. Railway deploys main to production and the staging branch to staging, so a feature branch has no preview URL. The command defaults the webhook to https://thetimeline.cc/api/webhooks/polar. That route has to be deployed before Polar deliveries succeed.

Environment variables

POLAR_ACCESS_TOKEN=
POLAR_SERVER=sandbox
POLAR_ORGANIZATION_ID=
POLAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET=
POLAR_WEBHOOK_ID=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_PAYG=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_TEAM=
POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_BUSINESS=
POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID=
POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE=
BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=false
PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAILS=

PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAILS is a comma-separated allowlist for /app/admin/billing and pnpm billing:operator. It is not a team role. Comped workspaces keep metering and the spend cap, skip Polar checkout and meter ingest, and show the comp on the team billing page. Price overrides (platform fee, included members, per-member price, metered discount percent) and a per-team Polar discount id are optional and can expire. Every change is appended to billing_operator_audit.

Products and meters (v1)

  • Timeline Pay as you go — €0 subscription. Checkout saves the card. Usage above the Free floor is ingested and invoiced monthly.
  • Timeline Team — €49/mo + up to €60 included usage discount
  • Timeline Business — €199/mo + up to €250 included usage discount

There is no prepaid wallet and no top-up product. pnpm billing:polar-setup archives a sandbox product named Timeline Top-up if one is still active.

Native Polar meters (event name → sum units):

  • timeline_ai_eur_cents — customer AI charge in euro cents
  • timeline_recall_minutes
  • timeline_email_units
  • timeline_storage_gb_month
  • timeline_accepted_sources

Extra active members accrue as member_days on the local spend cap (€2 per member-month) and do not consume Team/Business included-usage discount. Daily janitor charges settle each extra member once per day and use a cumulative monthly cent delta so rounding still totals €2. That meter is not ingested to Polar and is not a Polar invoice line.

Agent turns, concurrent Recall bots, custom MCP servers, documents, storage, indexed chunks, and active-member ceilings are plan capacity enforced in product code. Do not add Polar meters for them. /app/usage, Billing settings, and /pricing keep those ceilings in a closed Infrastructure limits disclosure rather than the default billed view. Webhook and search bursts stay on Redis token buckets; unique ingest volume is accepted_sources. A person may claim one Free grant after email verification; failed claims retry on the next signed-in load. Extra owned workspaces are restricted until a payment method is added. Unverified owners are not granted Free by the backfill migration. Removed owner memberships cannot claim or steal that person-level grant.

Background workers attach a billing context so OpenRouter wrappers settle the ai meter from exact usage.cost. Ask and Recall keep their own reserve/settle path and skip the wrapper meter to avoid double-counting.

Webhook

Point a Polar webhook at POST /api/webhooks/polar with Standard Webhooks / whsec_ signing. Subscribe at least to subscription, order, and refund events (order.paid, order.refunded, refund.created, refund.updated, plus subscription.created, subscription.active, subscription.updated, subscription.canceled, and subscription.revoked). pnpm billing:polar-setup creates that endpoint. Keep it pointed at a deploy that serves this route; production and staging only follow main and staging. Webhook signatures require a timestamp within five minutes. Subscription events compare Polar modified_at to polar_event_modified_at, not local updated_at, after locking the billing account in the same transaction. Request bodies are bounded to the integration webhook size limit before signature verify. Paid-plan activation writes the catalog default spend cap (€25/€100/€500) when the workspace is still on Free; later subscription updates preserve an administrator-selected paid cap, including €0 as a hard stop. Settlement that exceeds the paid spend cap still records the full ledger charge and freezes the workspace. Raising a positive spend cap restores read_only or balance_exhausted to the plan's active state. past_due and payment_retry stay paused until a later subscription webhook returns an active state. Entering past_due emails owners once per UTC month. Extra member-days persist a denied charge and freeze rather than skipping the extra seat. Janitor member-day ticks retry unsettled facts across the current period / 31-day window after billing recovery. Terminal Recall no-shows settle elapsed waiting-room minutes; a retry still releases so the same meeting can reserve again. Cancelling a joining/active meeting with no transcript chunks settles elapsed waiting-room minutes instead of releasing them. Postmark success is recorded before email settlement. Meeting-finalize billing uses the system actor so a departed creator cannot skip settlement. Free-grant inserts use ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING so a unique race cannot abort team-create. The post-verify Free-grant backfill prefers oldest restricted Free workspaces and never assigns the grant to a paid team. Scheduled join billing denials fail the occurrence without pausing auto-join as consecutive failures. Document uploads are blocked in every non-reservable billing state. order.paid does not credit a local balance. Included invoice discount is collected locally and is not ingested. Polar receives the customer-payable remainder, with native units scaled by that remainder, including sub-cent rounds that stay local when invoice cents are 0. PAYG, Team, Business, and Enterprise meter events are ingested when a Polar customer exists and charges are live. Comps are not ingested. Polar ingest uses a ledger outbox that claims a row before ingesting, Polar receives a stable event id, and a failed ingest does not undo a committed charge. Janitor included-discount resets lock the billing account so they cannot overwrite a newer Polar plan or period. Ask streams that abort after work starts settle at least the reserved customer charge. Failed structured-output attempts still contribute OpenRouter usage.cost. Recall minutes that cross a UTC month are attributed to each month they span. Document create serializes capacity under advisory lock key 1; restore takes key 1 then key 2 so restored chunks serialize with extraction writes. Worker AI reservations count against plan costlyWorkerConcurrency and delay the job when busy. Background AI metering keeps the reservation when settle fails after the provider call and reuses the BullMQ job id. Aborting a Recall join after the bot is accepted keeps the reservation when leave fails so the janitor can retry. Terminal Recall settlement errors propagate so the status webhook can 503 and retry. Re-accepting a removed member stamps a new membership createdAt and appends the previous interval to prior_intervals. A leftover Free team stays unrestricted while another owner's grant remains assigned. Storage snapshots backfill missed days. Slack/Telegram/MCP Ask and web chat also keep the reservation when settlement fails. Paid-plan changes reuse the Polar subscription in non-active paid states. Extra-member plan preview prorates from period member-days, including person-days when the current plan has no extras. Inbound email audio skipped for a denied email meter is flushed by the janitor when billing can reserve again. Denied inbound-email parent enrichment is stamped billing_enrichment_deferred and flushed on the email meter, not the accepted-source meter. Deferred accepted-source flush rotates past still-blocked rows; a replay after an insert crash meters the existing dedup row. Seat claims are blocked while billing is paused. Enterprise settlement does not freeze on the spend cap, and Enterprise plan changes go through support or the Polar customer portal. Background AI metering skips OpenRouter when the durable operation is already settled or the reservation is reused with provider_completed. Member-day ledger rows stamp plan_id; later ticks reuse that day's included-member count. Storage-cap finalization deletes the uploaded object when admission fails. Captured Slack/Telegram/email attachments assert document and storage capacity. Free in-allowance usage is not invoiced when live charging is enabled. Janitor Recall leave retries run before reservation expiry, and expiry skips rows with pending_recall_leave_bot_id. Interactive search embeddings run under billing ALS. The Free grant unassigns when its owner leaves or is demoted. Member-day janitor ticks backfill missed days. Deleted documents are excluded from indexed-chunk capacity; restore counts those chunks. Web chat settles known compression cost when the answer stream fails. BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=false is a process-wide shadow kill switch. Set BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED on document-extract-orchestrator as well as web/worker; it is a non-secret allowlisted toggle, not an extra credential.

Public and app surfaces

  • Public catalog: /pricing
  • Team usage: /app/usage
  • Team settings: /app/team?section=billing (owner/admin) — plan, PAYG checkout (a €0 Polar subscription that saves the card; usage above the Free floor is invoiced monthly), Team and Business checkout on the same subscription, spend cap (hard stop and 50/75/90/100 alerts), and cheapest-plan preview only when another self-serve plan saves at least €5 and 10% of the current projected bill. Selecting another paid plan updates the existing Polar subscription (or the customer portal) instead of opening a second checkout, including when the workspace is past due, in payment retry, or read-only. past_due and payment_retry show a pause banner and an Update payment method button that opens the Polar customer portal. Enterprise workspaces do not show PAYG/Team/Business checkout buttons. Submitted Polar discount codes must match POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE before POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID is attached.
  • Owner emails: spend-cap 50/75/90/100%, Free near-limit / exhaustion, and subscription past_due (billing_usage_alert via Postmark; once per threshold or past-due notice per period)
