Polar billing setup

Timeline uses Polar as merchant of record for optional Team / Business subscriptions, pay-as-you-go checkout, and native usage meters. Customer charges stay in shadow billing until BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=true.

Environments

  • Sandbox dashboard: sandbox.polar.sh · API https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1
  • Production dashboard: polar.sh · API https://api.polar.sh/v1

Sandbox accounts, tokens, products, and meters are fully isolated from production. Create a Timeline-specific access token; do not reuse Vernix / Irmin product or webhook IDs even when they share a Polar organization.

Products and meters (v1)

  • Timeline Pay as you go — €0 platform fee + metered overage
  • Timeline Team — €49/mo + up to €60 included usage discount
  • Timeline Business — €199/mo + up to €250 included usage discount
  • Timeline prepaid top-up — one-time €10 wallet credit (first PAYG collection)

Native Polar meters (event name → sum units):

  • timeline_ai_eur_cents — customer AI charge in euro cents
  • timeline_recall_minutes
  • timeline_email_units
  • timeline_storage_gb_month
  • timeline_accepted_sources

Extra active members accrue as member_days on the prepaid wallet ledger (€2 per member-month) and do not consume Team/Business included-usage discount. Daily janitor charges settle each extra member once per day and use a cumulative monthly cent delta so rounding still totals €2. That meter is not ingested to Polar and is not a Polar invoice line.

Agent turns, concurrent Recall bots, custom MCP servers, documents, storage, indexed chunks, and active-member ceilings are plan capacity enforced in product code. Do not add Polar meters for them. /app/usage, Billing settings, and /pricing keep those ceilings in a closed Infrastructure limits disclosure rather than the default billed view. Webhook and search bursts stay on Redis token buckets; unique ingest volume is accepted_sources. A person may claim one Free grant after email verification; failed claims retry on the next signed-in load. Extra owned workspaces are restricted until a payment method is added. Unverified owners are not granted Free by the backfill migration. Removed owner memberships cannot claim or steal that person-level grant.

Background workers attach a billing context so OpenRouter wrappers settle the ai meter from exact usage.cost. Ask and Recall keep their own reserve/settle path and skip the wrapper meter to avoid double-counting.

Webhook

Point a Polar webhook at POST /api/webhooks/polar with Standard Webhooks / whsec_ signing. Subscribe at least to subscription, order, and refund events (order.paid, order.refunded, refund.created, refund.updated). Keep the endpoint disabled until the URL is publicly reachable (local tunnel or deploy). Webhook signatures require a timestamp within five minutes. Subscription events compare Polar modified_at to polar_event_modified_at, not local updated_at, after locking the billing account in the same transaction. Request bodies are bounded to the integration webhook size limit before signature verify. Paid-plan activation writes the catalog default spend cap (€25/€100/€500) when the workspace is still on Free; later subscription updates preserve an administrator-selected paid cap, including €0 as a hard stop. Settlement that exceeds the reserved wallet or paid spend cap still records the full ledger charge and freezes the workspace. A €10 top-up first collects outstanding wallet shortfalls, then credits any remainder and restores the plan's active state only when remaining debt is 0 (and the catalog default cap when a shortfall freeze had zeroed it). Extra member-days persist a denied charge and freeze rather than skipping the extra seat. Janitor member-day ticks retry unsettled facts across the current period / 31-day window after billing recovery. Terminal Recall no-shows settle elapsed waiting-room minutes; a retry still releases so the same meeting can reserve again. Cancelling a joining/active meeting with no transcript chunks settles elapsed waiting-room minutes instead of releasing them. Postmark success is recorded before email settlement. Meeting-finalize billing uses the system actor so a departed creator cannot skip settlement. Free-grant inserts use ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING so a unique race cannot abort team-create. The post-verify Free-grant backfill prefers oldest restricted Free workspaces and never assigns the grant to a paid team. Scheduled join billing denials fail the occurrence without pausing auto-join as consecutive failures. Document uploads are blocked in every non-reservable billing state. Refunds of the €10 top-up debit the prepaid wallet, serialize concurrent refund.created / order.refunded totals, and freeze the workspace if that credit was already spent. Refunds that arrive before order.paid are stored and applied when the top-up credits. Local usage settlement is independent of Polar ingest: prepaid wallet and included discount already collect locally, so those units are not sent to Polar meters. Remaining Polar ingest uses a ledger outbox that claims a row before ingesting, Polar receives a stable event id, and a failed ingest does not undo a committed charge. Janitor included-discount resets lock the billing account so they cannot overwrite a newer Polar plan or period. Ask streams that abort after work starts settle at least the reserved customer charge. Failed structured-output attempts still contribute OpenRouter usage.cost. Recall minutes that cross a UTC month are attributed to each month they span. Document create serializes capacity under advisory lock key 1; restore takes key 1 then key 2 so restored chunks serialize with extraction writes. Worker AI reservations count against plan costlyWorkerConcurrency and delay the job when busy. Background AI metering keeps the reservation when settle fails after the provider call and reuses the BullMQ job id. Aborting a Recall join after the bot is accepted keeps the reservation when leave fails so the janitor can retry. Terminal Recall settlement errors propagate so the status webhook can 503 and retry. Re-accepting a removed member stamps a new membership createdAt and appends the previous interval to prior_intervals. A leftover Free team stays unrestricted while another owner's grant remains assigned. Storage snapshots backfill missed days. Slack/Telegram/MCP Ask and web chat also keep the reservation when settlement fails. Paid-plan changes reuse the Polar subscription in non-active paid states. Extra-member plan preview prorates from period member-days, including person-days when the current plan has no extras. Auto-reload retries owner notification after delivery failure without a second Polar checkout. Inbound email audio skipped for a denied email meter is flushed by the janitor when billing can reserve again. Denied inbound-email parent enrichment is stamped billing_enrichment_deferred and flushed on the email meter, not the accepted-source meter. Deferred accepted-source flush rotates past still-blocked rows; a replay after an insert crash meters the existing dedup row. Seat claims are blocked while billing is paused. Enterprise settlement skips prepaid freezes and does not debit a prepaid wallet so Polar meters can ingest. Enterprise is not a prepaid-wallet plan, so Billing does not offer top-ups or auto-reload and does not open a second self-serve checkout; plan changes go through support or the Polar customer portal. Background AI metering skips OpenRouter when the durable operation is already settled or the reservation is reused with provider_completed. Member-day ledger rows stamp plan_id; later ticks reuse that day's included-member count. Refund reversals persist wallet_shortfall_cents before freezing; a spend-cap raise does not restore the paid active state while that debt remains. Storage-cap finalization deletes the uploaded object when admission fails. Captured Slack/Telegram/email attachments assert document and storage capacity. Free in-allowance usage is not wallet-billable when live charging is enabled. Janitor Recall leave retries run before reservation expiry, and expiry skips rows with pending_recall_leave_bot_id. Interactive search embeddings run under billing ALS. The Free grant unassigns when its owner leaves or is demoted. Member-day janitor ticks backfill missed days. Deleted documents are excluded from indexed-chunk capacity; restore counts those chunks. Web chat settles known compression cost when the answer stream fails. BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED=false is a process-wide shadow kill switch. Set BILLING_CHARGES_ENABLED on document-extract-orchestrator as well as web/worker; it is a non-secret allowlisted toggle, not an extra credential.

Public and app surfaces

  • Public catalog: /pricing
  • Team usage: /app/usage
  • Team settings: /app/team?section=billing (owner/admin) — plan, €10 top-up on wallet-backed plans, auto-reload Polar checkout email (Polar checkout is marked created before owner mail; delivery failure retries the same URL the same UTC day without a second checkout; skipped unless remaining spend-cap headroom covers the full €10 product, including a €0 spend cap), cheapest-plan preview, spend cap. Selecting another paid plan updates the existing Polar subscription (or the customer portal) instead of opening a second checkout, including when the workspace is past due, in payment retry, or read-only. Enterprise workspaces do not show PAYG/Team/Business checkout buttons. Submitted Polar discount codes must match POLAR_DISCOUNT_CODE before POLAR_DISCOUNT_ID is attached.
  • Owner emails: spend-cap 50/75/90/100% and Free near-limit / exhaustion (billing_usage_alert via Postmark; once per threshold per period)
