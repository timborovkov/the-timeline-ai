The work becomes the record.

Timeline captures work as it happens, then generates cited updates, digests, handoffs, and answers from the evidence.

No new process.

No manual reporting. No "where was that decided?" archaeology.

The Timeline 01

The old way is breaking

Teams do the work, then separately document that the work happened.

Meetings create decisions, but the decision disappears into notes.

Slack creates commitments, but status still has to be written later.

CRMs and trackers only know what someone remembered to update.

Stakeholder updates, handoffs, CRM hygiene, and project reporting become duplicate labor.
The Timeline 02

Timeline removes the second pass

Your team keeps communicating and shipping. Timeline turns that activity into the operational record.

  • Capture Slack, meetings, docs, email, tasks, and customer conversations.
  • Preserve raw evidence so every answer can point back to the source.
  • Extract updates, commitments, decisions, risks, and project/client memory.
  • Let people ask what happened without interrupting the team.

Why teams trust it

Approval-backed state
